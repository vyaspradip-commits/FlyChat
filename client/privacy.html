📄 Privacy Policy

FlyChat respects your privacy. This policy explains what information FlyChat uses and how it is handled while you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

FlyChat may store information required for account and messaging features, including your name, email address, profile information and chat data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide FlyChat features such as account login, user profiles, private messaging, group messaging and privacy controls.

3. Messages

Messages are stored so that conversations can be displayed to the sender and recipient when they return to a conversation.

4. Privacy Controls

FlyChat provides privacy controls that allow you to manage profile visibility, last-seen visibility, online status and read receipts.

5. Profile Visibility

Your profile visibility setting determines which users can view your available profile information.

6. Last Seen and Online Status

FlyChat may provide controls for managing the visibility of your last-seen and online status.

7. Read Receipts

When read receipts are enabled, other users may be able to see when their messages have been read.

8. Data Sharing

FlyChat does not intentionally share your personal information with other users except when required for application features such as profiles and message delivery.

9. Data Security

FlyChat uses application-level security measures intended to protect user information. Users should not share passwords or other sensitive information through chat.

10. Account and Data Deletion

If account or data deletion functionality is available in FlyChat, users can use the available account controls to manage their data.

📜 Terms & Conditions

1. Acceptable Use

Users should use FlyChat responsibly and must not use the application for unlawful, abusive or harmful activities.

2. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for the information and messages they send through FlyChat.

3. Privacy Settings

Users are responsible for reviewing and selecting their preferred privacy settings.

4. Service Availability

FlyChat may be modified, updated or temporarily unavailable while development and maintenance are being performed.

5. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FlyChat features or data-handling practices change.
